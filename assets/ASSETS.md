# Lute Roll assets

Borrowed from my College of Keys repo (https://github.com/kiyoshithekidd/college-of-keys), same paths under assets/images/. These are copies for inspiration and reuse. Do not copy the College of Keys repo itself.

## Status

The PNG files are staged locally at ~/workspace/lute-roll/assets/ (19 files, about 43 MB unoptimized). They are NOT in this GitHub repo yet: the GitHub connector here only pushes UTF-8 text, so binary PNGs cannot go through it. Pull them from the College of Keys repo with the raw URLs below, or from the staged local copies, then resize and compress before use. The PRD requires a small bundle and no heavy asset on first load.

Most files are 1 to 5 MB originals at 2048 px or similar. Resize for mobile web (tray and backdrops to at most 1024 px wide, icons to at most 256 px), compress, and only ship the ones the screen actually uses.
